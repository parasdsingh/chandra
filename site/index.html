macOS menu bar

The sky, where you already look.

A jyotisha calendar that lives in the menu bar. The Moon's phase at a glance, a panchanga for any day, and a Lagna Kundali that redraws itself as the lagna moves — all sixteen divisional charts. Computed on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere.

macOS 11 Big Sur or later.

What it does

Nine calendars, a panchanga, and a chart — each with its own menu bar item, so what you want is one click away and what you don't is not there at all.

01

The Moon, always visible

The current phase is drawn in the menu bar itself, not hidden behind a click. The calendar beneath it gives every day of the month its phase.

02

Nine grahas, nine calendars

A month of transits for any graha — rashi and nakshatra ingresses, stations, combustion — each switchable into the menu bar on its own.

03

A full panchanga

Tithi, vara, nakshatra with pada, yoga, karana and the muhurtas, for the day you pick. Amanta or purnimanta, or a plain solar month.

04

Sixteen divisional charts

The shodasavarga, from D1 to D60, Parashari throughout. Any of them can sit in the menu bar beside the others.

05

A chart that moves

Each graha stands at its own degree along a route through its house, and the whole ring advances as the lagna crosses its sign. A reading of now that looks like one.

06

Offline, and private

Swiss Ephemeris runs on your Mac. There is no account, no telemetry and no network code in the app at all — your location never leaves the machine.

What it is built on

The figures come from somewhere, and the app says where. Every position is Swiss Ephemeris; outside its precise range the chart says so on its face.
